1)DEVELOPMENT OF PROBLEM STATEMENT:

AIM:
Develop problem statement for Passport Automation Statement.


PROBLEM STATEMENT:
In the present passport application system applicants have to visit passport office for registrationof slot,verification of 
documents and they have to wait for their turn which wastes their time.


SOLUTION:
The Passport Automation System provides a website where applicants can register, apply,upload documents, pay fees, and track the 
passport status.


OBJECTIVES:
*To reduce manual work.
*To increase the ease to apply a passport.
*Tracking the status of their passport.
*To increase security.

MODULES:
*Registration
*Login
*Uploading documents
*Payment
*Tracking status
*Verification


RESULT:
Problem statement is developed.
